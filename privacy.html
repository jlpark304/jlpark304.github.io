KinderBot privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

KinderBot is a personal tool used only by its owner (jlpark304@gmail.com). It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: it reads school-related emails and their attachments in the owner's account, and adds a label to emails it has processed.
  • Google Calendar: it creates and updates school events in the owner's calendar.

How the data is used

Email content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API only to pull out dates, events and to-dos. The results are written to the owner's Google Calendar and Skylight family calendar, and a short summary is sent to the two parents by WhatsApp. The data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage

KinderBot does not keep its own database of email content. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
